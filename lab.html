Ersatz Labs is an independent Second Life workshop making small tools for the awkward gaps between what you want to do and what the interface remembered to provide.

The laboratory's governing method is straightforward: notice an annoyance, ask a dangerous question, build something, press it repeatedly until it stops exploding.
