01 / PLACES

REMEMBER WHERE

Save locations that matter, organise them into useful categories, keep favourites close and clear unwanted recent locations when they no longer deserve the space.

02 / PEOPLE

REMEMBER WHO

Keep your own private record of people worth remembering, including favourites, notes, recent encounters and direct routes to profile and contact.

03 / PRESENCE

RECONNECT

Presence cues for quick reconnection. Shown only for people you've chosen — no passive monitoring.

04 / NOTES

LEAVE A CLUE

Add context while it is still in your head. Because “I'll remember that” remains one of virtual life's least reliable storage systems.

OPERATING PRINCIPLE / 03

BUILT TO
DISAPPEAR.

Magellan is meant to reduce friction, not become another thing demanding attention. Its interface is compact, menu-driven and designed around quick actions.

It keeps the user's choices at the centre: saved people are explicitly chosen, places can be forgotten, and the device is intended as a personal memory aid rather than a tracking system.
